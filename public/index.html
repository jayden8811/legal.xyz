How privilege works

  1. Your lawyer directs it

    Your lawyer signs an agreement that makes legal.xyz their agent. Courts have long extended privilege to experts working at a lawyer's direction.

  2. It stays about legal advice

    Conversations are organized by legal matter, marked privileged, and flagged when they drift into business topics that privilege doesn't cover.

  3. It's ready to defend

    If a request ever comes, your lawyer already has the signed agreement and a privilege log to assert the claim.

Whether a communication is privileged is decided by a court. legal.xyz is designed to give you the strongest possible claim, not a guarantee.

Questions

Is legal.xyz subpoena-proof?

No product can be. A subpoena to us returns only encrypted data, because we never hold your key. Your company can still be asked for its own chats; your lawyer can then claim privilege, and a court decides.

Why does my lawyer have to sign?

Privilege covers communications made to get legal advice from a lawyer. A federal court found in 2026 that AI chats used without a lawyer weren't privileged. Your lawyer's direction is what gives you a real claim.

Can legal.xyz read my chats?

No. They're encrypted on your device with a key protected by your password and recovery code, and we never receive either.

What if I forget my password?

Use your recovery code. If both are lost, nobody can recover your chats, including us.

What happens when a lawsuit starts?

Your lawyer places a legal hold, which pauses auto-delete, and can export a privilege log of protected conversations.
